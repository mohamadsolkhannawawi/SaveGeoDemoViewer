## Recording Notes

- **Microphone:** Condenser (cardioid pattern preferred)
- **Room tone:** Acoustically treated, minimal ambient noise
- **Peak levels:** –3 dB to –6 dB
- **Delivery:** Warm, conversational but authoritative. Clear articulation of Indonesian terms (AOI, biomassa, tutupan lahan).
- **Pacing:** Allow natural breath between major sections. Title cards (0:00–0:03, etc.) have no narration; use silence for visual emphasis.
- **Narration pauses:** Position narration to start ~0.5 seconds after scene transition, end ~1 second before next scene.
